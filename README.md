## How to install

1. Click the purple button above. A file called `Elio.dmg` appears in your Downloads.
2. Open it and double-click Elio.
3. Click **Install**. Elio moves itself into your Applications folder and opens.

The first time, your Mac may ask whether you want to open an app downloaded from the internet. Click **Open**: Elio is checked by Apple.

That's all. Elio updates itself, so you never need to come back here.

## Not sure your Mac can run it?

Click the Apple menu in the top-left corner of your screen, then **About This Mac**. You need a chip whose name starts with **Apple M** and **macOS 26** or later.
